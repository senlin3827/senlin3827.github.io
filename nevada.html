How This Nevada Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Nevada by subtracting federal income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. Nevada does not have a state income tax. This means there is no state-level deduction taken out of your paycheck, resulting in a higher take-home amount compared to most other states.

Nevada vs. High-Tax States (California)

Why do so many people move to Nevada? Because Nevada has 0% state income tax. If you are relocating from a high-tax state like California, you will immediately notice a larger paycheck. Earning a $100,000 salary in Las Vegas means you avoid the state-level withholdings you would face in Los Angeles.

Nevada Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Nevada have state income tax?

No! Nevada is one of the few states without a state income tax. Your paycheck is only subject to federal taxes.

Are there local income taxes in Nevada?

No. Nevada does not impose local or city income taxes.

Is it better to work in Nevada or California financially?

Purely from an income tax perspective, Nevada is much better. California has a top state income tax rate of 13.3%, while Nevada has 0%.
