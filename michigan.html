How This Michigan Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Michigan. It subtracts federal income tax, Michigan state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary.

State income tax: Michigan has a flat tax rate of 4.25%.

Federal tax: Based on 2025 federal tax brackets.

FICA: 6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare.
