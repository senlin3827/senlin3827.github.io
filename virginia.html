How This Virginia Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Virginia by subtracting federal income tax, Virginia state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. Virginia uses a progressive state income tax system with rates ranging from 2% to 5.75%.

Virginia vs. Neighboring States (DC & Maryland)

How does Virginia compare? Virginia's top income tax rate is 5.75%. In contrast, neighboring Washington DC has a top rate of 10.75%, and Maryland also has a top rate of 5.75%. Because many people work in DC but live in Virginia, understanding the tax reciprocity and commuter rules is essential for maximizing your take-home pay.

Virginia Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Virginia have state income tax?

Yes. Virginia has a progressive state income tax system with rates ranging from 2% to 5.75%.

Are there local income taxes in Virginia?

No. Virginia does not impose local or city income taxes on wages, making it simpler than states like New York or Ohio.

Do I pay DC tax if I live in Virginia?

If you live in Virginia but work in DC, you generally pay DC income tax on your wages, but you may claim a credit on your Virginia return to avoid being double-taxed.
