How This New Jersey Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in New Jersey by subtracting federal income tax, New Jersey state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. New Jersey uses a progressive state income tax system. Additionally, New Jersey has some of the highest property taxes in the nation, though they do not directly affect your paycheck withholding.

New Jersey vs. Neighboring States

How does New Jersey compare? New Jersey has a progressive income tax, with top rates reaching 10.75%. In contrast, neighboring Pennsylvania has a flat 3.07% rate. If you live in New Jersey but work in Pennsylvania (or vice versa), you may face complex tax filing requirements, which can impact your overall take-home pay.

New Jersey Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does New Jersey have state income tax?

Yes. New Jersey has a progressive state income tax system with rates ranging from 1.4% to 10.75%.

Are there local income taxes in New Jersey?

No. New Jersey does not impose local or city income taxes on wages. All employees pay the state rate based on their income level.

Is it better to work in New Jersey or Pennsylvania financially?

It depends on your salary. Pennsylvania has a flat 3.07% rate, which is lower for high earners.
