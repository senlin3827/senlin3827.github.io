Why Use the NYC-Specific Calculator?

Most generic New York paycheck calculators only account for state income tax. However, if you live in New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, or Staten Island), you are subject to an additional local income tax. Our calculator factors this in, giving you a much more accurate estimate of your take-home pay.

If you live in Upstate New York or Long Island, you should use our standard New York Paycheck Calculator instead.

New York City Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does NYC have an additional local income tax?

Yes. Residents of New York City must pay a local income tax ranging from roughly 3.078% to 3.876%, on top of the NY state income tax.

How much does living in NYC cost compared to upstate NY?

Because of the NYC local income tax, take-home pay for the same salary in NYC is lower than in upstate New York.

Do I pay NYC tax if I only work in the city but live outside?

No. The NYC local tax is generally based on your residency, not just your workplace. If you live in New Jersey or Upstate NY, you do not pay NYC local income tax.
