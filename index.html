How This DC Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Washington DC. It subtracts federal income tax, DC state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary.

DC income tax rates: 4% to 10.75% depending on income level.

Federal tax: Based on 2025 federal tax brackets.

FICA: 6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare.

DC Paycheck Calculator FAQ

What is the average take-home pay in DC?

It depends on your salary and filing status. This calculator gives you an instant estimate.

Does DC have state income tax?

Yes. DC has its own income tax system with rates from 4% to 10.75%.

What is FICA?

FICA includes Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%).
