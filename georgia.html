How This Georgia Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Georgia by subtracting federal income tax, Georgia state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. Georgia has moved to a flat state income tax rate of approximately 5.19%. This flat rate simplifies calculations compared to states with complex progressive tiers.

Planning to relocate? Compare your take-home pay with the Florida Paycheck Calculator (0% state tax) or the Texas Paycheck Calculator (0% state tax).

Georgia vs. No-Tax States (Florida)

How does Georgia compare? Georgia has a flat 5.19% state income tax. In contrast, neighboring Florida has 0% state income tax. If you live in Atlanta, your take-home pay will be lower than earning the same amount in Miami due to the state tax deduction.

Georgia Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Georgia have state income tax?

Yes. Georgia has a flat state income tax rate of approximately 5.19%.

Are there local income taxes in Georgia?

No. Georgia does not impose local or city income taxes on wages.

Is Georgia a high-tax state?

Georgia has a moderate flat tax rate of 5.19%, which is higher than Texas (0%) but lower than California's top rate of 13.3%.
