How This Comparison Works

This tool calculates your estimated net take-home pay in both New York and Florida, accounting for federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare. Florida has 0% state income tax, while New York has a progressive tax system with rates up to 10.9%, plus additional local taxes if you live in New York City. The difference in your paycheck can be massive.

Use this comparison if you are considering a move between New York and Florida, especially if you are retiring or working remotely.

New York vs Florida Paycheck Comparison FAQ

How much more take-home pay do you get in Florida vs New York?

On a $100,000 salary, a Florida resident takes home significantly more per year than a New York City resident, primarily because Florida has no state income tax and no local city tax.

Does Florida have state income tax?

No. Florida does not have a state income tax, while New York has a top marginal rate of 10.9% plus local tax.

Is it worth moving to Florida for the tax savings?

It depends on your salary and lifestyle. For high earners and retirees, the annual tax savings can be substantial. Use the calculator above to see your exact difference.
