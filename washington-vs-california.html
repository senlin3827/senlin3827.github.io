How This Comparison Works

This tool calculates your estimated net take-home pay in both Washington and California, accounting for federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare. Washington has 0% state income tax, while California has a progressive tax system with rates up to 13.3%. The difference in your paycheck can be massive.

Use this comparison if you are considering a move between Seattle and San Francisco, or if you are working in tech and negotiating a remote salary.

Washington vs California Paycheck Comparison FAQ

How much more take-home pay do you get in Washington vs California?

On a $100,000 salary, a Washington resident takes home significantly more per year than a California resident, primarily because Washington has no state income tax.

Does Washington have state income tax?

No. Washington does not have a state income tax, while California has a top marginal rate of 13.3%.
