How This New York Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in New York by subtracting federal income tax, New York state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. New York uses a progressive tax system with top rates reaching nearly 10.9%. Additionally, if you work or live in New York City (NYC), you are subject to an additional local income tax, which significantly reduces your take-home pay compared to upstate New York.

New York vs. No-Tax States (Texas & Florida)

How does New York compare? New York has one of the highest combined tax burdens in the country. In stark contrast, states like Texas and Florida have 0% state income tax. If you are considering relocating for a job, earning $100,000 in New York City will leave you with significantly less take-home pay than earning the exact same salary in Austin or Miami.

New York Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does New York City have a local income tax?

Yes. Residents of NYC are subject to a local income tax ranging from roughly 3.078% to 3.876%, on top of the New York state income tax. This calculator's simplified state tax estimate accounts for this combined burden.

What are the state income tax rates in New York?

New York has a progressive tax system with rates ranging from 4% to 10.9%, depending on your income level and filing status.

Are there any special deductions in New York?

Yes, employees in New York may be subject to State Disability Insurance (SDI) and Paid Family Leave (PFL) deductions, which are small but mandatory payroll withholdings unique to the state.
