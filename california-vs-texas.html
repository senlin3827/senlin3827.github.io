How This Comparison Works

This tool calculates your estimated net take-home pay in both California and Texas, accounting for federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare. Texas has 0% state income tax, while California has a progressive tax system with rates up to 13.3%. The difference in your paycheck can be substantial.

Use this comparison if you are considering a move between California and Texas, or if you are negotiating a remote work salary across state lines.

California vs Texas Paycheck Comparison FAQ

How much more take-home pay do you get in Texas vs California?

On a $100,000 salary, a Texas resident takes home about $6,386 more per year than a California resident, mainly because Texas has no state income tax.

Does Texas have state income tax?

No. Texas does not have a state income tax, while California has a top marginal rate of 13.3%.

Is it worth moving to Texas for the tax savings?
