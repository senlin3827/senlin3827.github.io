How This Florida Paycheck Calculator Works

This calculator estimates your take-home pay in Florida by subtracting federal income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. Florida does not have a state income tax. Because there are no state or local income taxes, your paycheck calculation is simplified, and your take-home pay is higher compared to states like California or New York.

Florida vs. States with Income Tax

Why do so many Americans move to Florida? Because Florida has 0% state income tax. If you are relocating from a high-tax state like New York, you will immediately notice a larger paycheck. Earning a $100,000 salary in Miami means you avoid the state-level withholdings you would face in New York City, allowing you to keep more of your hard-earned money.

Florida Paycheck Calculator FAQ

Does Florida have state income tax?

No! Florida is one of the few states that does not impose a state income tax on individuals. Your paycheck is only subject to federal taxes (FICA and federal income tax).

Are there local income taxes in Florida?

No. Florida does not have any city or local income taxes. This makes calculating your take-home pay very straightforward.

How does Florida compare to Texas financially?

Both Florida and Texas are fantastic for your paycheck because neither state collects state income tax. Your take-home pay in Florida will generally be the same as in Texas, assuming the same salary and federal filing status.
